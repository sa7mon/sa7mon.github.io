I spoke a few weeks ago at SecretCon! I meant to put up a post about it ahead of time, but I had a lot of work to do getting the slides in order that it didn't happen. This is the second year in a row I have given a talk there - you can read about last year's talk [here](posts/i-am-speaking-at-secretcon-2025/). This year's talk was about running Tor relays, something I've done for a few years but haven't written much about.

Once the videos are published, I'll update this post with a link. Until then, here is some metadata about the presentation as well as the slides.

## Title

From User to Operator: How to Run a Tor Relay and Defend Online Freedom

## Abstract

Privacy is a fundamental human right. By running a Tor relay, you can contribute to a global communication network, free from spying and authoritarian censorship.

In this session, I’ll describe my journey going from a Tor user to becoming volunteer relay operator. I’ll walk through my experiences working directly with users in censored countries to get around blocks and explain how you can help by running your own relay and bridges. I’ll explain the real-world challenges relay operators face and how those challenges are addressed in practice.

The talk will cover the "why", but more importantly the "how": how someone with a technical background can create, monitor, and maintain a Tor relay as well as stay connected with other operators.
